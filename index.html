How it works

Shipwatch runs the tests, checks every deploy and watches the live page.

  1. Connect your repo. One sign-in, no config file.
  2. Merge as usual. Shipwatch follows the pull request, the build and the deploy.
  3. Get one message. It tells you what changed on the live page, or what broke and where.

What you get

Checks before the merge

A failing check stops the merge, and you see the failing step in plain words.

A deploy you can see

Every deploy is followed to the end. A stuck build is reported, not forgotten.

The live page, read back

After the deploy, Shipwatch opens the page and confirms your change is really there.
